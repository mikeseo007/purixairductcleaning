How Much Does Dryer Vent Cleaning Cost in Miami?

By Purix Air Duct & Dryer vent cleaning Editorial Team  ·  3 min read

The cost of dryer vent cleaning varies because every vent run is different. Expect the final price to reflect how long the duct is, how accessible it is, and whether the technician finds blockages or damaged ductwork that need repair. This guide explains what shapes the cost, what you can expect during a visit, and how to decide if a professional cleaning is worth it for your home.

TL;DR

  • Dryer vent cleaning cost depends more on access and duct complexity than on the brand of dryer.
  • Regular cleaning reduces fire risk and keeps the dryer working efficiently.
  • Ask for a written scope and any recommended repairs before agreeing to additional work.
Typical cost driverslength of duct, accessibility, amount of lint and obstructions
Average job timemany homes take 45 to 90 minutes; complex runs take longer
Safety benefitclean vents reduce fire risk and improve dryer efficiency

Cost factors that change what you pay

These factors explain why two nearby homes can see very different quotes for the same basic dryer vent service.

Duct length and complexity

Longer runs, multiple bends, or runs through walls and ceilings add labor and may require specialty tools.

Accessibility

Exterior access directly behind the dryer is cheaper than crawling into an attic or removing drywall to reach the vent.

Level of buildup or blockage

A lightly linted vent is a quick clean; bird nests or heavy compacted lint take more time and may need repairs.

Need for repairs or parts

Torn ductwork, broken vent caps, or replaced connectors add materials and labor to the job.

Vent termination type

Rigid metal ducts are easier to clean than flexible foil ducting, which can trap lint and require careful handling.

How professional cleaning helps performance and safety

A properly cleaned vent improves airflow, which shortens drying cycles and can reduce energy use from your dryer. More important, removing lint and debris reduces a significant fire hazard. Technicians also spot damage that can leak combustible lint into hidden cavities or allow pests to build nests in the ductwork.

If you are weighing the cost, consider these practical benefits:

  • Faster drying saves money over time
  • Reduced likelihood of dryer-related fires
  • Potentially longer appliance life due to reduced heat stress

Those benefits are the main reason many homeowners treat vent cleaning as routine maintenance rather than an optional expense.

Choosing who should clean your vent

Not every handyman or general cleaner has the right tools for a full dryer vent cleaning. A trained dryer vent technician uses rotary brushes sized to the duct, high-capacity vacuums, and sometimes video inspection to ensure the entire run is cleared. When selecting a provider, ask about their process, what tools they use, and whether they will inspect both the interior run and exterior termination.

If you prefer to learn about the company before contacting them, see Purix Air Duct & Dryer vent cleaning for an overview of their air duct and dryer services.

What a typical professional session looks like

This timeline helps you set expectations for a single-unit dryer vent cleaning.

  1. 1Initial inspection
    Technician checks exterior termination, measures duct length, and looks for obstructions or damage.
  2. 2Dryer disconnect and setup
    Dryer is moved and disconnected safely. Tools and containment are positioned to keep lint out of the room.
  3. 3Mechanical cleaning
    Rotary brushes and vacuums clear lint from the full run. If needed, the exterior hood is cleared as well.
  4. 4Final inspection and testing
    Technician inspects the cleared duct, may run the dryer to check airflow, and reports any recommended repairs.

Next steps and scheduling an inspection

Start by documenting where your vent terminates and any symptoms you have observed. If the termination is hard to access or the run goes through an attic, mention that when you request a quote. When the technician arrives, expect a brief inspection, a recommended scope of work, and a written estimate before any repairs are performed.

To schedule a professional inspection and get a no-surprise quote, contact Purix Air Duct & Dryer vent cleaning via their website or by phone. A local inspection is the best way to convert the general cost guidance here into an exact number for your home.

WARNING: When to call a pro right away

Watch for this: If you detect smoke, a strong burning smell, or the dryer becomes hot to the touch while cycling, stop using the dryer and call a professional immediately. These are signs of severe airflow restriction or an electrical issue that needs prompt attention.

How to prepare for a scheduled cleaning

Simple steps you can take to reduce time on site and avoid surprises.

  • ✓Clear 3 to 5 feet around the dryer so the technician can work and move the appliance
  • ✓Empty lint traps and any nearby laundry baskets
  • ✓Note any recent issues, such as long drying times or unusual smells
  • ✓Make sure exterior vent access is not blocked by furniture, plants, or debris
  • ✓If you have pets, secure them so they stay out of the work area

Frequently asked questions

Can I clean my dryer ducts myself?

You can perform basic cleaning steps yourself, such as removing lint from the dryer trap and clearing the exterior vent flap. However, a homeowner cleaning often misses lint trapped deep in a long run or behind bends. A professional uses rotary brushes, vacuums, and sometimes video cameras to clean the entire duct. If you attempt a DIY clean, do not use a household vacuum alone for deep runs and avoid damaging foil or flexible ducts, which can make problems worse.

How long does it take a professional to clean a dryer vent?

The time varies with duct length and accessibility. Many single-unit jobs finish in about 45 to 90 minutes, while runs through attics, with heavy blockages, or needing repairs take longer. A technician will give a time estimate after an initial inspection at your property.

How often should dryer vents be cleaned?

Frequency depends on your household. For many homes, every 1 to 2 years is typical. If you have large loads, a gas dryer, pets that shed, or notice longer drying times, consider annual cleaning. A technician can recommend a schedule after seeing the condition of your ductwork.

Can a handyman clean a dryer vent?

A handyman may be able to remove visible lint and clear an exterior vent, but thorough dryer vent cleaning requires specific tools and knowledge to clear the entire duct run and to identify damage. For a complete cleaning that improves safety and airflow, a specialist who offers dedicated dryer vent services is the safer choice.
