You turn on the AC and the house fills with a damp, stale, "old basement" smell. In humid places like Cape Coral, Naples, Fort Lauderdale and Boca Raton, it's one of the most common indoor air complaints we hear. The good news: the cause can usually be found and fixed.

## What causes a musty AC smell?

A musty odor almost always comes down to **moisture plus organic material**. Your AC removes humidity from the air, and that moisture has to go somewhere. When dust, pet dander and debris inside the system stay damp, mold and bacteria can grow, and the blower spreads that smell through every vent.

Common sources include:

- **Dirty ductwork.** Dust that collects inside ducts holds moisture and gives microbes something to feed on.
- **Condensation on cold ducts.** Ducts running through a hot attic can sweat, especially if insulation is damaged.
- **A clogged condensate drain.** If the drain line or pan backs up, standing water sits right next to the air handler.
- **A home that sat closed up.** Seasonal homes, or homes with the AC set high while you travel, can develop odors quickly.
- **Past leaks or storm damage.** Water that got into the system or attic can leave lingering contamination.

## Is it mold in the air ducts?

Not always, but a persistent musty smell, dark spots around supply vents, or allergy symptoms that get worse indoors are signs worth checking out. A professional inspection can tell you whether the issue is in the ductwork, the air handler or somewhere else.

## How to get rid of a musty AC smell

1. **Replace the air filter.** It's the easiest first step, but it rarely solves the problem on its own.
2. **Check the condensate drain.** Make sure it's flowing and the drain pan isn't holding water.
3. **Control indoor humidity.** Keep the thermostat at a steady setting, and avoid shutting the AC completely off for long periods in summer.
4. **Have the ducts professionally cleaned.** Removing the dust and debris that holds moisture removes the source of the odor.
5. **Sanitize after cleaning.** An [air duct sanitizing](/air-duct-sanitizing/) treatment after cleaning helps reduce odor-causing bacteria and mold particles left on duct surfaces.

Air fresheners and vent clips only mask the smell. If it keeps coming back, the source is still there.
